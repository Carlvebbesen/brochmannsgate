## Entry: how the measurements fit (round 3, in the model)
Photo 38 is taken looking **west**. The owner confirmed that the tiles end between the kott door and the front door.
- The back wall is the bathroom wall (A). It has 0.89 of wall (*badevegg*, with a light switch), then the bathroom door, which opens out into the entry.
- The left wall is the coat-hook wall (C, *knaggvegg* 1.01). This checks out: 1.01 + 1.102 corridor ≈ living-room width minus the bathroom.
- The open door on the right is the front door, hinged next to the bathroom corner. The kott door is further east in the same wall.
- The tiles run 0.958 out from the bathroom wall and 1.662 from the coat-hook wall to the front-door wall.
  So the entry, and with it the bathroom, is **1.662 deep**. The drawing's 1.31 was wrong.
- Consequence: the Hovedsoverom door can't sit at the measured 0.597 / 1.244 (its frame would reach 7.51, past the entry's
  north wall at 7.33). The 0.88 frame is kept and slid south. See [05-open-questions.md](05-open-questions.md).
