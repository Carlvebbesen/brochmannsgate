## Layout (north = up on the plan)
- **Stue/Kjøkken** (bottom-left): 5.488 × 3.475 living part. The kitchen is in the upper-left, next to the balcony.
  Large 4-pane window facing west, balcony door in the corner.
- **Balkong**: inset in the north-west corner, with a curved (bowed) railing. The building is on its east side
  (kitchen and bathroom windows) and its south side (balcony door).
- **Bad**: north of the kitchen, window onto the balcony.
- **Entré**: front door on the north side, near the bathroom. A 1.102 m hallway runs south between the kitchen wall and
  the bedroom wall and opens into the living room under a beam.
- **Kott**: small empty closet (0.79 × 0.94) next to the front door.
- **Bedroom column** on the east facade, from north to south: Hovedsoverom, Kontor, Tvstue (owner's names).

Room areas in the model: Stue/Kjøkken 26.9, Entré 4.1, Bad 2.6, Kott 0.7, Hovedsoverom 8.2, Kontor 8.3, Tvstue 9.3 and Balkong 6.5 m².
The sum (≈60 m² plus internal walls) is a little under the 66 m² BRA-i, as expected when BRA-i is measured to the exterior walls.
