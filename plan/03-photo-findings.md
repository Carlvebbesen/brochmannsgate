## Per-room summary

### Stue/Kjøkken (Living/Kitchen, open plan, bottom-left on plan)
- **Floor**: Light oak plank parquet, straight-lay, warm honey-blonde tone (~#D9BD8E base, matte satin finish). Continues from kitchen through living/dining into entré and bedrooms — one continuous floor throughout apartment except bathroom/kott/entryway tile.
- **Walls**: Sage/greige — a soft grey-green, roughly #B9C0B4 to #C3C8BC depending on light (lighter and cooler in daylight photos, warmer/greener under tungsten lamp light in evening-styled shots).
- **Ceiling**: Flat white (#F5F5F2), painted plaster, standard flat ceiling with white cornice/crown moulding (simple ogee profile, ~7-10cm) at wall-ceiling junction throughout apartment. Recessed round downlights (matte white bezel, warm white light) scattered through kitchen and living areas — no beams, no stucco medallions, no visible slope in this main room (looks like standard flat pre-war ceiling, height reads as generous, likely ~2.6-2.7m based on window proportions).
- **Windows**: Living/dining window is a wide 4-panel white-painted timber casement bank with horizontal glazing bar dividing upper/lower panes (period-style, "korspost"-like but wider), deep white masonry sill/reveal at approx. seat height, radiator tucked below. Kitchen window is a 2-panel casement, same style, above the sink.
- **Kitchen**: Matte grey/graphite (anthracite) flat-front cabinetry, slim integrated bar-pull handles, light warm-grey stone-look laminate/quartz countertop, U-shaped run plus an island with black induction hob, stainless double wall ovens stacked, slotted extractor hood recessed into upper cabinet run, undercabinet the same oak floor as living room (note: one photo shows a slightly different honey-toned bamboo-look floor patch near the stove — may be a different area rug/mat, not a different material).
- **Doors**: White painted flat/paneled interior doors with simple casings, chrome lever handles.
- **Balcony access**: A tall white-framed glazed door (single leaf) leads from living room directly onto the curved balcony — matches the drawn bulge on the floor plan's left edge.
- **Skirting**: White, plain ogee/torus profile, ~8-10cm, matches cornice detailing.

### Balkong
- Distinct curved (bowed) shape confirmed exactly as drawn on the plan (rounded bay). Railing: painted white/cream tubular steel — a mix of horizontal rail bars along the curved section and closer vertical spindles further round; solid painted balustrade knee-wall below rail line (not full glass — opaque render/stucco up to ~90cm, then open rail above). Floor: dark grey/brown-stained timber decking boards. Ceiling/soffit above: painted cream plaster, continuous with facade color, with a rolled/curved edge. View: mature trees and neighboring cream apartment buildings (Bjølsenparken green backdrop, leafy, west/south-facing based on evening sun angle in photos).

### Bad (Bathroom, top-middle-left)
- **Floor & walls**: Large-format beige/greige tile (looks like a warm concrete/microcement-effect porcelain tile, matte, minimal grout lines), floor-to-ceiling on walls, same tile continues to floor — narrow galley room matching the plan's slim rectangle.

- **Window**: One small aluminum/steel-look top-hung casement window with textured/reeded obscure glass at the far end, white painted reveal.
- **No bathtub** — walk-in shower only.

- **Bedroom B (middle)**: similar sage-moss green (~#8A9884), same window style, decorative framed prints above bed, door opens directly toward dining table (confirms this bedroom door is the one nearest kitchen/entré per plan).
- **Bedroom C (bottom, plan-right, smallest per plan dimensions 3.03×3.08m corner)**: walls read slightly cooler/greyer-blue (~#A9B0AC), navy bedspread staging, built-in frosted-glass sliding wardrobe along one wall, small decorative arched doorway/niche detail leading to a dressing corner with mirror — an architectural feature (arch) not indicated on the flat floor plan, worth adding as a nice detail if desired but not structural.
- All bedroom windows: single or double-pane white casement, deep white sills, simple design, consistent with living/kitchen windows but slightly narrower (single or double vs. the wider multi-light living/kitchen windows).

## Contradictions / clarifications vs. floor plan
- The floor plan shows a plain rectangular "Kott"; photos show it fitted out with a full-height mirrored/frosted sliding wardrobe on one wall — worth modeling as built-in storage, not empty closet.
- One bedroom (photo 33) shows a decorative arched opening/niche near the wardrobe corner that isn't indicated on the flat 2D plan — a cosmetic/architectural detail, optional to include.
- The balcony's curved (bowed) footprint in the floor plan is fully confirmed by photos — it's a true curved balcony, not just a stylized plan render, with the railing curving in two "radius" sections (tighter near living room corner, wider sweep at the far end) exactly as drawn.
- Kitchen appears in a U-shape plus a peninsula/island (not a straight run) — the plan's simple L-shaped counter outline under the sink window is confirmed, and the island with induction hob (drawn as a small square/cooktop symbol) matches the "four burner" icon in the plan.
- Entrance floor material: photos show a small distinct tiled zone right inside the front door (not indicated as different material on plan, but common in Oslo apartments) — otherwise oak flooring is continuous through the whole apartment per photos.
- No bathtub anywhere — confirms Bad is shower-only as implied by the plan's shower icon.
